How speed changes within each chunk

Normalize each actual execution window to 0–100% time and divide it into 12 equal-duration bins. Lines show per-bin medians across chunks; bands show the 25th–75th percentiles. R = mean speed in the last ⅓ ÷ first ⅓; R ≤ 0.8 means a slowdown of at least 20%.

Positions come from offline robot TCP FK. Adjacent feedback samples use actual tick times to compute speed, aggregated with time weights. No differentiation across chunk boundaries or extra smoothing is applied. Bands show percentile ranges, not confidence intervals.

The end of a saved trajectory is not necessarily executed

The entire saved canonical_raw trajectory is also normalized to 0–100% below. It has already been decoded by the action adapter and may include cropping and handoff processing; it is not the raw model tensor. Its late segment is usually replaced by the next replan.

Sensitivity check: is the change limited to the start of handoff?

Exclude the first 50 / 100 ms of each executed chunk, then compare the first and last ⅓ of the remaining window. Also resample positions into 12 equal-duration speed segments and apply a 3-point moving average to inspect feedback quantization effects. Each rollout is shown separately.

Chunks are correlated; these are descriptive statistics for this recording batch. Changes after excluding the start provide clues but cannot alone identify waypoint processing, RTC or the model as the cause.
