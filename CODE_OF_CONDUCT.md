# Code of Conduct

ManiMux adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies to the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and project community
channels, including the developer WeChat group.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected and unacceptable behavior

Be respectful, give and accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the
community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments,
sexualized language or imagery, publishing others' private information without permission,
and other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
setting.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers of
[SII-LiuLab/manimux](https://github.com/SII-LiuLab/manimux) (the repository administrators).
All reports are reviewed promptly and confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject
contributions and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban participants, following the
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
of the Contributor Covenant.
